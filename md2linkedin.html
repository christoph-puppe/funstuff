◬ Caveats worth knowing

Accessibility: screen readers announce 𝗺𝗮𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗺𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗯𝗼𝗹𝗱 letter-by-letter as "mathematical bold small a…" or skip them entirely. Never style a whole post — use it on 2–4 anchor phrases.
Search: LinkedIn's index treats 𝗞𝘂𝗯𝗲𝗿𝗻𝗲𝘁𝗲𝘀 and Kubernetes as different strings. Keep keywords unstyled.
Coverage: the Unicode math planes cover A–Z a–z 0–9 only. Everything else — umlauts, accents, ß, punctuation — passes through unchanged.
